## Welcome to The Sankara Hill Penida

Discover a sanctuary of serenity where modern luxury meets breathtaking ocean views. Nestled on the hills of Nusa Penida, The Sankara Hill Penida offers an exclusive escape, designed for travelers seeking comfort, privacy, and unforgettable moments.

### Facilities Overview
A collection of refined facilities crafted to elevate comfort and well-being, complemented by a tranquil hilltop setting and panoramic ocean views. From arrival to dining, wellness, and curated experiences, every element is designed to enhance a seamless and elevated island stay.

### Sankara Hill Penida Lobby
**Open 24 Hours**
A warm yet sophisticated arrival experience, where contemporary design meets panoramic views of the surrounding hills and ocean, creating a welcoming sense of place.

### Puṇṇa Restaurant
**Opening Hours:** 07.00 – 22.00
**In-room & In-villa Dining:** 07.00 – 22.00
Designed for refined all-day dining overlooking the picturesque hills and ocean of Nusa Penida, Puṇṇa Restaurant offers elegant indoor and alfresco seating options. The restaurant presents a thoughtfully curated selection of Indonesian and international Mediterranean cuisines, crafted from fresh, locally sourced ingredients to deliver a memorable culinary experience from breakfast through dinner.

### Phala Pool & Bar
**Open daily from** 10.00 – 22.00
Sip on an array of refreshing beverages and handcrafted cocktails while basking in the island sun or unwinding after a day of exploration. Adjacent to Puṇṇa Restaurant and the infinity pool, Phala Pool & Bar offers a relaxed yet sophisticated setting overlooking the ocean horizon — perfect for leisurely afternoons and captivating sunsets.

### Radha Spa Hill Penida
**Open daily from** 11.00 AM – 10.00 PM
A tranquil wellness sanctuary offering holistic treatments inspired by Balinese healing traditions. Guided by experienced therapists, guests are invited to restore balance and relaxation in a serene hilltop setting.
